# Operations Questions

**Project:** PACE ICE Volunteer Access SOP  
**Role:** PM  
**Organization:** HAAG  

---

## Recurring Tasks

The main recurring task has been keeping track of which researchers need access to the PACE ICE cluster, especially around the start of a new semester. This includes checking in with project leads, making sure required information is collected, and confirming that requests are submitted correctly.

Since I joined this group at a later stage, part of my role has also been making sure things stay on track with existing timelines. This involves checking in on progress, helping out wherever necessary, and ensuring deadlines are being met.

A large portion of my focus has been on documentation and ensuring that my subgroup is aligned with the overall process.

---

## Assessing My Role as Project Manager

The approach in this role has been fairly structured but still somewhat hands off. Since I joined the project later, the goal has not been to change the direction of the team, but to support existing progress and help push things toward completion, especially with the goal of publication.

The focus has been on defining and improving the workflow, documenting it clearly, and ensuring that my subgroup is staying on track. Input is given when needed, but the team largely continues to operate independently.

Overall, the role has been more about reinforcing structure, helping where necessary, and making sure deadlines are being met rather than directly managing every part of the project.

---

## Challenges and Successes

**Successes**
- Clear SOP created  
- Improved organization  
- Progress toward publication  

**Challenges**
- Joined late

---

## Collaborating with Stakeholders

Collaboration with stakeholders has been fairly light and indirect. Most interaction has been through project leads and researchers requesting access, rather than constant direct communication with PACE administrators.

Since joining later, the focus has been on supporting existing communication structures rather than redefining them. The main role has been ensuring that stakeholders have clear documentation and that requests are being handled properly.

---

## Feedback Received

There has not been much direct feedback on the PM role so far. Most feedback has been indirect through how people interact with the SOP and documentation.

Some confusion around required information suggests that documentation can still be improved. At the same time, more consistent request submissions indicate that the system is working.

---

## Plans for Enhancing My Role

Going forward, the main focus is continuing to support the team in reaching a publication and ensuring that deadlines are met.

This includes:
- Improving clarity of documentation  
- Increasing adoption of the SOP  
- Helping the subgroup stay aligned with project goals  
- Stepping in when necessary to keep progress on track  

There is also an opportunity to make the process more visible so users naturally follow it.

---

## Support Needed

Nothing major is needed at this point. The role has been manageable, especially since the team is relatively independent.

Additional support in improving visibility of the SOP and potential automation tools could help strengthen the system further. The main priority moving forward is maintaining progress and ensuring successful completion of the project.

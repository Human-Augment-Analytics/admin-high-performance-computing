# Instruction for validated-pace-users Slack channel
Thu Le - HPC Initiative - Spring 2026

## Scope of Initiative
One of the main challenges within HAAG’s Slack workspace is that PACE and HPC-related resources are fragmented across channels, making it difficult for student researchers to find timely and practical support. When issues arise, students often rely on posting questions in general channels. This leads to delayed responses, lost information, and repeated questions. This limits efficiency and creates unnecessary barriers for students navigating complex computational resources.

To address that issue, this initiative creates a structured, student-led Slack channel dedicated to PACE and HPC support within the HAAG organization. Instead of introducing a new platform, the initiative leverages Slack, which is already widely used and familiar to HAAG members, ensuring low barriers to adoption. The primary participants are student researchers, with a designated administrator responsible for moderation and oversight. The goal is to centralize communication and foster a collaborative peer-learning environment where students can both seek and provide help.

## Alignment with HAAG’s Goals
This initiative aligns with HAAG’s goals of supporting student research and fostering interdisciplinary collaboration.  This student-driven initiative aims to strengthen community engagement and encourage shared responsibility in solving HPC problems. Through observations of Slack activity, it was evident that students frequently encountered challenges with PACE but lacked a centralized space for discussion and troubleshooting. By creating a dedicated and structured space for these interactions, this initiative supports HAAG’s mission to enhance research productivity and build a more connected student research community.

## Procedures and Purpose
The primary procedure developed for this initiative includes a structured protocol for user admission and community engagement within the validated-pace-users Slack channel. 

1) The admission protocol ensures that members have a basic level of preparedness in using PACE, which helps maintain the quality and relevance of discussions.
2) The engagement protocol is to encourage active participation, particularly from more experienced users, to sustain a collaborative environment.

The purpose of these procedures is to create a well-organized, supportive, and sustainable peer-learning space where students can efficiently navigate PACE resources and contribute to collective knowledge. The intended audience for these procedures includes both the dedicated HAAG admin responsible for managing membership and structure, as well as student users who participate in the community.

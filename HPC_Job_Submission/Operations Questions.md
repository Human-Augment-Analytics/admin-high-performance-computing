# Operations Questions

**Project:** LIDAR
**Role:** PM
**Organization:** HAAG

---

## Recurring Tasks

The main recurring task is monitoring the team's Discord to verify that work is being completed according to the milestones that have been set. This includes checking in with the co manager to reflect on meetings and track overall progress. Attending team meetings periodically has also been part of the routine not to direct the work, but to observe what is being discussed and confirm that the project is moving forward.

---

## Assessing My Role as Project Manager

The approach taken in this role has been intentionally hands off. Since the team has been working together for a while and has a strong understanding of the project, it has not been necessary to interject frequently. Input and direction are offered when warranted, but the team largely drives itself.

Within this structure, the role has been split with a co manager, the co manager tends to work more closely with the technical team and stakeholders, while the focus on my end has been progress tracking and check-ins. Attending meetings on occasion has been more than enough to get a clear picture of where things stand.

Overall, the role has been handled well given the nature of the team and the stage of the project.

---

## Challenges and Successes

**Successes**

- The team is highly self sufficient and has continued making consistent progress without needing significant direction
- Work is actively moving toward a publication, which reflects well on the group's output
- A GitHub and HAAG portal question that came up was resolved quickly by checking in with John and noting the outcome

**Challenges**

- Because the team operates independently, it can be difficult to establish a clear PM presence without overstepping
